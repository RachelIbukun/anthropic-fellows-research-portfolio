# Research Roadmap

## Long-Term Goal

Develop evidence-based approaches for understanding the security, governance, and societal implications of advanced AI systems.

---

# Phase 1 – Foundations

Current Focus

### AI Security

* AI-assisted software development
* Secure AI deployment
* Software supply-chain security
* Security debt

### Economics & Policy

* Future of work
* Skill formation
* Productivity versus security trade-offs
* Organisational resilience

Outputs

* Literature reviews
* Research journal
* Initial benchmark design

---

# Phase 2 – Experimental Research

Objectives

* Build benchmark environments
* Evaluate AI-assisted development workflows
* Compare productivity and security outcomes
* Develop security risk scoring models

Outputs

* Public datasets
* Evaluation framework
* Research reports

---

# Phase 3 – Governance & Policy

Objectives

* Develop governance recommendations
* Explore organisational AI adoption frameworks
* Analyse deployment risks

Outputs

* Policy briefs
* Governance playbooks
* Public research articles

---

# Phase 4 – Fellowship-Level Research

Potential Topics

* Security Risks in AI-Assisted Software Development Pipelines
* Productivity vs Security Trade-offs
* AI Governance for Engineering Organisations
* Early Warning Indicators for Unsafe AI Deployment

---

# Success Criteria

Produce practical research that helps organisations deploy increasingly capable AI systems more securely, responsibly, and effectively.

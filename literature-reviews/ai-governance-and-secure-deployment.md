# Literature Review

## Topic

AI Governance and Secure Deployment

## Overview

As AI systems become increasingly capable and are deployed into operational environments, governance becomes as important as capability.

The challenge is no longer simply building powerful systems; it is ensuring those systems are deployed safely, responsibly, and accountably.

## Core Governance Questions

### Accountability

Who is responsible when AI assisted decisions result in harm, security incidents, or operational failures?

### Oversight

What level of human review should be required before deployment?

### Transparency

How should organisations document the use of AI systems in engineering and decision making workflows?

### Risk Management

How should organisations evaluate risks introduced by AI systems before deployment?

## Organisational Controls

Common governance mechanisms include:

* Human review requirements
* Audit logging
* Risk assessments
* Change management controls
* Security evaluation frameworks

## AI Security Perspective

Governance and security are closely linked.

Strong governance improves:

* Accountability
* Traceability
* Incident response
* Risk ownership

Weak governance can amplify security risks even when technical controls are present.

## Relevance to My Research

The proposed research on AI assisted software development sits directly at the intersection of:

* AI Security
* Governance
* Organisational resilience
* Public policy

Understanding governance requirements is therefore essential for evaluating secure AI deployment.

## Personal Reflection

As AI systems become more deeply integrated into critical workflows, governance should be viewed not as a constraint on innovation but as an enabling mechanism for trustworthy deployment.

# Future Research Directions

This document captures potential future projects and open questions.

---

## AI Security

### Security Risks in AI-Assisted Software Development

Investigate vulnerabilities introduced through AI-generated code, infrastructure configurations, and dependency recommendations.

---

## Frontier Model Evaluation

### Behaviour Under Adversarial Conditions

Explore how frontier models behave when exposed to conflicting objectives, ambiguity, and adversarial prompts.

---

## Economics & Policy

### AI and Expertise Development

Investigate how AI assistance affects skill formation, decision-making, and professional expertise.

---

### Productivity Versus Security

Develop frameworks for measuring the trade-offs between increased productivity and increased security risk.

---

## Governance

### Responsible AI Deployment

Explore governance mechanisms that support safe deployment of advanced AI systems within organisations.

---

## Organisational Resilience

### AI-Augmented Workforces

Investigate how increasing dependence on AI affects organisational resilience, adaptability, and long-term capability development.

---

## Long-Term Vision

Contribute practical research that helps ensure increasingly capable AI systems remain secure, trustworthy, and beneficial to society.
